How to read the COP

Important notes before using historical scores for school choices.

What does COP mean?

The Cut-Off Point is the PSLE score of the last student admitted to a school/course in that year's Secondary 1 Posting Exercise. It is determined by that cohort's results and school choices, and can change from year to year.

PG3 and IP are different

PG3 and IP values are shown separately. Do not compare them as if they were one common admission route. Some schools or specialised pathways may not have a directly comparable PG3/IP figure.

N/A is not zero

N/A means the source table does not provide a corresponding public COP figure for that school and year. It does not mean the score is zero or that the school has no admission route. “DSA” is retained where the source table records DSA.

Use as a guide, not a promise

Meeting or having a better score than a previous year's COP does not guarantee admission. School choice order, citizenship, tie-breakers, vacancies and each year's cohort can affect posting outcomes.
